ABOUT

BUT WHAT IS MUSE?

Muse was created by us for busy girls. We found that all of our friends had huge amount of cosmetics in their bag that it came to a point where it just became unorganized. Yet, why did everyone have to carry so many products just to touch up their lashes and lips? It just seemed to inconvinent, a mess, a stress. So this is where we decided to step up and bring a little something new to the cosmetic market.

We are a cosmetic brand that aims to create products which not only raise confidence but promotes practicality. Our product is an interchangeable dual ended touchup stick. The product itself will be two tubes that are connected by a 3D printed connector piece, which will hold the tubes together. With the changeability of the product, customers will be able to choose any 2 tubes from our range of lip oils, lip glosses, and mascaras to have a combo that is best for them.
